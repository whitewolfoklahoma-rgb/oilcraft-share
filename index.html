Help & Safety

How the dilution math works

Drops of essential oil = bottle size (ml) × dilution % × drops-per-ml ÷ 100. OilCraft defaults to 20 drops per ml. Drop size honestly varies with the oil and the dropper — thin oils can run closer to 30 drops/ml, thick ones fewer — so you can change the standard in Settings. Charts from the Tisserand Institute (built on a finer dropper) show about 6 drops for 2% in a 10 ml roller; at 20 drops/ml OilCraft says 4 — erring weaker is the safe direction.

Strength guide (conservative, Tisserand-based)

StrengthWho / what for
0.5%Young kids, roughly 3 months–6 years — cautious band. Under age 2, get professional guidance; never apply near an infant's face.
1%Kids 6+, facial products, daily-use adult blends, sensitive skin.
2%Standard adult body blends — the everyday default.
3%Adults, short-term, one targeted area.
5%Adults, strong and short-term only. Not for daily use.

Safety rules that always apply

• Always dilute before skin use, and patch test first.
• Phototoxic oils (cold-pressed bergamot, lemon, lime): keep leave-on amounts low and avoid sun/UV on that skin for 12–18 hours.
• "Hot" oils (cinnamon bark, clove, oregano, thyme): use tiny amounts, always well diluted.
• Peppermint and eucalyptus: keep away from the faces of young children (peppermint under ~6, eucalyptus under ~10).
• Wintergreen is not for children. Tea tree is toxic to pets if swallowed or used undiluted — never apply oils to pets.
• Diffuse in ventilated rooms and let pets leave the room if they want to.
• OilCraft gives no ingestion advice. Don't use essential oils internally based on this app.
• Pregnant, nursing, taking medication, or blending for babies? Ask a qualified aromatherapist or your care provider.
• Oxidized (past-best) oils are more likely to irritate skin — move them to cleaning and diffusing duty.
